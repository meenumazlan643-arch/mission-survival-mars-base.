MISSION CONTROL // BRIEFING

Welcome, Commander.

Your crew has landed on Mars. Keep oxygen, water, food and power above zero for 10 days. Each day, choose one maintenance action, then advance time. Watch your resources: survival depends on balance.

Tip: solar maintenance restores energy; water recycling and oxygen tuning use energy. Some days bring random events.
